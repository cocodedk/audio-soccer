Audio Soccer trial

Put on headphones. This short test checks one thing: can you find the ball, take it to the goal and score using only your ears? You always face the goal you attack. The ball rattles when it rolls and ticks when it is still, the goal chimes every two seconds while you have the ball, and the side boards knock when you touch them. A voice tells you what happens.

  • Space kick up the pitch; in the attacking third it shoots at the goal, and Left or Right aims inside that post
