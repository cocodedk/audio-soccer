# Privacy Policy: Audio Soccer

**App:** Audio Soccer (`dk.cocode.soccer`)
**Developer:** CoCode.dk, Babak Bandpey
**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

**Audio Soccer collects nothing.** It does not collect, send or share any personal data. It needs no account, and it has
no network permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.

## What stays on your phone

The app keeps two settings on your phone: the language (English or Persian) and the speech engine you chose. They are
kept in a private data store that only this app can read. The app never sends them anywhere. Deleting the app or clearing
its data removes them. The app also tells Android not to back them up, so they go neither to a cloud backup nor to a new phone.

## Permissions

The app asks for one permission, to vibrate the phone during play. Android grants it when you install the app and never
asks you about it. An Android library also adds one permission of its own, which only apps signed with the same key as
this one can hold. It gives no access to your data, your phone or the internet.

## Speech

The game speaks through a speech engine installed on your phone, such as eSpeak, and passes it the words to say. What
that engine does with the words is up to the engine and its own privacy policy: some engines can use an online voice, for
example. You choose the engine in the game's Settings. The game itself never sends the words anywhere.

## No tracking

There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no third-party tracking tools and no advertising identifiers.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
