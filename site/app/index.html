Audio Soccer, a football game you play by ear

Audio Soccer is a free Android app made for blind and low-vision players. The game tells you everything with sound and speech, so you play with the screen reader turned off.

Get the app

The app is not out yet. We are preparing its first release and its listing on F-Droid, a free app store for Android. This page will link to both as soon as they are ready.

Until then, you can try the game in a web browser. The browser trial needs a computer with a keyboard. On a phone, the phone test lets you try the touch controls.

  • Try the browser trial
  • Open the phone test

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer.
  • A speech engine, such as eSpeak NG. The game speaks through the speech engines installed on your phone, and you choose one in Settings. If your phone has none, install one first.

You play on the phone's own speaker, and the game needs no internet connection.

How a game goes

You play with the screen reader turned off, and the game speaks for itself in English or Persian. There are three levels: find the ball, score a goal and beat the keeper. Each level has three rounds.

  1. Set up the game with your screen reader on or off. With it on, the menus are ordinary buttons that your screen reader reads. With it off, the game speaks its own menus.
  2. Choose a level. If your screen reader is still on, the game asks you to turn it off, and waits.
  3. Play the three rounds. A fanfare plays for each round you win.
  4. Hear how you did. Each level remembers your best time and whether you won all three rounds. The level's menu option tells you both, and the summary tells you when you set a new record.
  5. When you are done, turn your screen reader back on yourself.

Gestures

In the menus, with the screen reader off

  • Hold a finger on the screen to hear an option.
  • Swipe to move between options: quickly with one finger, or at any speed with two.
  • Tap once to hear the current option again.
  • Double-tap to choose it.

During play

  • Drag a finger anywhere on the screen to run. Up the screen is toward the goal.
  • Tap to kick.
  • Tap with two fingers to hear where you are.
  • Tap with three fingers to pause.

The phone vibrates when the ball comes into reach and when you line up with the goal.

If you can see some of the screen

The menus show white text on black, with the option in focus in yellow. During play, the pitch is drawn in bright colours on black: you in cyan, the ball in orange, the keeper in pink and the goal in yellow.

Privacy

Audio Soccer collects no personal data. It has no internet permission, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your settings and records stay on your phone. They are not backed up, and they go when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
