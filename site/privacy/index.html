Privacy policy for Audio Soccer

Audio Soccer collects nothing. It does not collect, send or share any personal data. It needs no account, and it has no network permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.

App
Audio Soccer (dk.cocode.soccer)
Developer
Cocode (opens in a new tab), Babak Bandpey
Last updated
18 November 2026

What stays on your phone

The app keeps two settings on your phone, the language (English or Persian) and the speech engine you chose, and your records: the best time in each level and the levels where you won all three rounds. They are kept in a private data store that only this app can read. The app never sends them anywhere. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes them. The app also tells Android not to back them up, so they go neither to a cloud backup nor to a new phone.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission, to vibrate the phone during play. Android grants it when you install the app and never asks you about it. An Android library also adds one permission of its own, which only apps signed with the same key as this one can hold. It gives no access to your data, your phone or the internet.

Speech

The game speaks through a speech engine installed on your phone, such as eSpeak NG, and passes it the words to say. What that engine does with the words is up to the engine and its own privacy policy: some engines can use an online voice, for example. You choose the engine in the game's Settings. The game itself never sends the words anywhere.

No tracking

There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no third-party tracking tools and no advertising identifiers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact
